# Baguette

- Hotbar slot 3 from the start of the run. Left click swings once and hits the closest police enemy within the swing volume and line of sight. The baguette is reusable and deals 10 damage per hit.
- A surviving cop is launched away from the player with an initial speed of 24 m/s and 6 m/s of lift. The stagger suspends the cop's AI for 0.65 seconds. The host applies damage and movement and replicates the existing police snapshots with the hit reaction.
- The model comes from [Kenney's CC0 Food Kit](https://kenney.nl/assets/food-kit), via [the Food Kit mirror](https://github.com/Hidencod/tge-assets/blob/3ddcc695adbba9fb918cf31b7c139b3f95b76910/packs/food-kit/loaf-baguette.glb). The GLB references the same color atlas as the Croissant.
